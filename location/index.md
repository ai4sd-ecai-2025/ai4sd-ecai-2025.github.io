> The Engineering School will host the **workshops, tutorials, and the doctoral consortium** on Saturday 25th and Sunday 26th October 2025. The headquarters of the Faculty of Engineering in Viale Risorgimento were designed by architect Giovanni Vaccaro and inaugurated on October 28, 1935. The building aims to be **both the symbol and the product of a renewed architectural culture and professional training**. It is a large functional building designed to meet the needs of study and research, with a red-brick tower which houses ancient and valuable books and mimics the city center Two Towers.
